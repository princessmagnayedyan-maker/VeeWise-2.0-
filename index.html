Common Types of Online Scams

Learn about different online threats and their warning signs.

✉️

Phishing Messages

Deceptive messages pretending to come from trusted organizations to obtain personal or financial information.

🛍️

Fake Online Shops

Fraudulent online stores that advertise products but may not deliver the purchased items.

💼

Fraudulent Job Offers

Fake employment opportunities that may ask applicants for suspicious payments or sensitive personal information.

📦

Fake Delivery Alerts

False delivery notifications that encourage users to open unfamiliar links or make unexpected payments.

👤

Impersonation Scams

Scammers pretending to be relatives, friends, companies, or government agencies.

💳

Payment Scams

Fraud involving false payment confirmations or suspicious requests for money transfers.

Online Safety Tips

Practice these habits to protect yourself online.

01. Think Before You Click

Avoid clicking unfamiliar links from unexpected messages.

02. Protect Your Passwords

Never share your passwords, OTPs, or verification codes.

03. Verify Information

Check whether the sender or organization is legitimate through official channels.

04. Be Careful with Online Payments

Review the seller and verify payments through your official payment application.

05. Avoid Rushed Decisions

Be cautious of messages that threaten you or pressure you to act immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VeeWise?

VeeWise is an educational website designed to help internet users recognize online scams and practice safer online habits.

Can the Scam Tester confirm a scam?

No. It only identifies possible warning signs using basic keyword detection. It cannot guarantee that a message is fraudulent or safe.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious message?

Avoid clicking unfamiliar links or sharing personal information. Verify the sender through official communication channels.

Does VeeWise collect my personal information?

This version does not collect or transmit information entered into the Scam Tester.
